The Villains

The film's demon characters are introduced as drawing on beings from Korean folklore, in particular the dokkaebi. In Korean stories dokkaebi sit between human and spirit — mischievous, fond of tricks and games, but generally not out to kill — which sets them apart from Western goblins or Japanese oni, despite the surface resemblance.

Movie Props

Hanbok

Korea's traditional dress. The women's version pairs a short jacket (jeogori) with a wrapped skirt (chima), together often called chima-jeogori; the men's version pairs a jeogori with wide-legged trousers (baji).

Gat

A general term for traditional Korean hats, most recognizably the wide black horsehair-and-bamboo hat worn by Joseon-era scholars and officials. Shape and material varied with the wearer's rank.

Jakhodo (鵲虎圖)

A genre of Joseon-era folk painting pairing a magpie and a tiger. Korea's wild tigers were hunted to local extinction by the early 20th century, but the tiger has stayed a recurring symbol in Korean art and storytelling ever since.

Gimbap

Rice and vegetables (often with meat or seafood) rolled in dried seaweed and sliced into bite-sized pieces — a portable, lunchbox-friendly dish closely tied to picnics and outdoor outings.

Seolleongtang

A milky-white soup made by simmering ox bones and brisket for hours. It's served unseasoned, with salt, pepper and chopped scallion added at the table to taste.

Ilwolobongdo (日月五峯圖)

A folding screen showing the sun, the moon and five peaks, traditionally placed behind the king's throne. The sun and moon stand for the king and queen, the five peaks for sacred ground — a classic emblem of royal authority in Joseon court painting. Originals can still be seen at Gyeongbokgung's Geunjeongjeon Hall, Changdeokgung's Injeongjeon Hall, and the National Palace Museum of Korea.

Mudang

The term for a shaman who officiates gut (shamanistic rites) in Korean folk religion. Mudang traditionally refers to a female shaman and baksu to a male one, with further regional terms such as danggol used for hereditary shaman lineages.
